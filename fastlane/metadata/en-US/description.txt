Plan dinner with the people you cook for.

Plated brings your weekly plan, recipes, groceries, and household together in one calm place. Everyone in your household can work from the same plan, while invited Table guests only see the dinners you choose to share.

PLAN THE WEEK
Schedule breakfast, lunch, dinner, or snacks. Add a recipe, write in takeout or leftovers, move meals as plans change, and mark what you actually cooked.

BUILD THE GROCERY LIST AUTOMATICALLY
Plated turns planned recipes into a combined shopping list, scales quantities for the servings you need, and keeps hand-added items and checkmarks intact. Export the list to Apple Reminders when you want it on your wrist or in another app.

KEEP YOUR REAL COOKBOOK
Save ingredients, instructions, photos, timing, tags, pantry staples, and favorites. Import recipes from a web link, pasted text, a scan, or a photo, then use Cook Mode for clear steps and timers.

COOK FOR YOUR HOUSEHOLD
Record dietary notes and ingredients to avoid. Plated flags recipes that need attention and leaves incompatible dishes out of suggestions.

SHARE A PRIVATE TABLE
Post a dinner, photo, comment, poll, or reaction to an invite-only Table. Reporting and blocking controls are built in, and a Table guest cannot access your household plan, groceries, or cookbook.

SEE YOUR RHYTHMS
Review what you cook most, how much variety you have, and which old favorites have fallen out of rotation. Plan gatherings, mirror them to Calendar, and get optional weather-aware meal suggestions.

MADE FOR APPLE DEVICES
Use Plated on iPhone and iPad, glance at home- and lock-screen widgets, ask Siri what is for dinner, and keep a cook timer visible with a Live Activity.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your recipes, plans, grocery lists, photos, household, and Table live in your iCloud and are shared only through invitations you control. Plated has no ads, analytics, or cross-app tracking. A small directory stores only the information needed for account matching, invitations, and notifications; details are available in the privacy policy.
