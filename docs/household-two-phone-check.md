# Household verification on Nate’s and Alessandra’s phones

Use Plated 1.0.1 (39) or newer on BOTH phones. Check the installed build in TestFlight. Keep both phones online and Plated open during each exchange. Allow up to two minutes as a diagnostic observation window, then refresh once more; this is not a delivery guarantee or a reason to keep retrying indefinitely.

## 1. Confirm the household, without removing anyone

Open your profile, then Household. Both phones should show Meadows with Nate as organizer and Alessandra as a joined member, not an outstanding invitation. Each person should be marked “You” only on their own phone. There should be one row per person.

If Alessandra is already joined, DO NOT remove her or rejoin just to repeat this test. Her existing membership can test photos and activity.

Only if she has not successfully joined: after BOTH phones update, Nate opens Household → Invite someone and generates a fresh invitation. Alessandra opens that exact new link and accepts. Do not resend the pre-update message or use an older link. If the sheet says removed, finish joining, or shows another error, capture the exact state rather than trying more invitations.

## 2. Check the in-app join notification separately

After a new acceptance, Nate opens Table and pulls down, then opens the bell. Expect one “Alessandra joined your household” row. Refresh again: it must not duplicate. Joining again through a genuinely new invitation after removal is a new event.

Build 39 can recover an unannounced organizer-side acceptance from the last 36 hours during its first full read. An older acceptance is not guaranteed to be backfilled. iOS banner permission, Focus and sound settings are separate from the in-app bell; an absent bell row is not explained by denied banner permission alone.

## 3. Verify Alessandra → Nate profile delivery

On Alessandra’s phone, open her profile → Edit profile. Confirm her intended avatar is selected. Append a temporary marker such as “Sync A” to her bio and tap Save. Open Table and pull down to publish/refresh.

On Nate’s phone, open Table and pull down. Open Household and tap Alessandra. Her bio should include “Sync A”; her photo should match the photo she sees. Check both the People row and the large profile avatar. Do not manually assign her photo from Nate’s phone.

If the bio arrives but the photo does not, report “bio yes, photo no.” If neither arrives, report that. They diagnose different failures. After both have build 39, Nate can also use Household → People → ••• → Refresh from iCloud for a complete reread. A successful read should say Household refreshed, not automatically open another invitation.

## 4. Verify Nate → Alessandra and persistence

Nate appends “Sync N” to his own bio and saves. Refresh Table on Nate’s phone, then Alessandra’s. Alessandra opens Nate’s profile and confirms the marker and his photo. Leave and reopen Plated on both phones; the photos and bios should remain correct. Remove the temporary bio markers and verify those edits arrive too.

## 5. Verify activity in both directions

Alessandra shares a Dish at the Table with a distinctive test title, such as “Sync check — Alessandra.” If Share with offers several Tables, select your shared household Table. Nate refreshes Table and confirms the exact post, author, and her “Activity · last 7 days” dishes-shared count increases by one.

Repeat with a dish from Nate and confirm it on Alessandra’s phone. This tests both publication and receipt; seeing your own post is not sufficient.

For cooked-dinner activity, use an actual newly shared household dinner: assign Alessandra as the cook, then mark it cooked when it is done. Refresh both phones. The same dinner should be marked cooked on both, with Alessandra credited once. Repeat with Nate’s next dinner to verify the other direction. Eating out does not count as a cooked dinner. Avoid fabricated cooked history merely to raise a counter.

Compare CHANGES from these new events, not absolute lifetime totals. Personal dinners from before joining remain private, so older totals can differ legitimately. Dinner counts cover the trailing seven calendar days; questions, reactions and grocery edits do not count as dishes shared.

## If anything fails

Stop at the first failed step. Record both installed build numbers, which phone made the change, approximate time, and the exact result (for example: bio arrived/photo missing, post arrived/count unchanged, or join succeeded/bell empty). Screenshots of both corresponding screens help. Do not reinstall, leave the household, or remove/reinvite as a troubleshooting loop.

These are live-account verification steps. Automated codec, merge, notification and queue tests cannot establish that the two real phones have successfully exchanged their records.
