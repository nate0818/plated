## Specific differences and constraints

1. **Real data:** original people, dishes, dates, counts, photos, instructions and notification text are illustrative. Native must use the actual record and its real state; no invented sample data in production. Missing image, absent optional metadata and denied permissions remain designed states.
2. **44-point targets:** target frames/content shapes are at least 44×44 even where the HTML concept uses 36pt text actions or smaller inline glyphs. Avatars/icons keep their approved visible sizes. Short actionable captions reserve real space rather than overlapping neighboring controls. This has to be rechecked in native trees and by off-text taps.
3. **Measured contrast correction:** cream secondary text is changed from approved `#67715E` to `#606A57`; dark is unchanged. Original secondary/fill and secondary/raised pairs measured 4.386:1 and 4.092:1. Corrected pairs are about 4.865:1 and 4.539:1. The original artifact is unchanged; this is a disclosed accessibility correction, not a claim of exact palette equality.
4. **iPad override:** Nate later requested horizontal only. Both landscape orientations are configured; original portrait-shaped iPad artwork is not an instruction to restore portrait. Narrow landscape windows/AX must still reflow without loss.
5. **Typography/CSS:** the final `.app .micro` override is 11px. Rendered tab labels are 15px because `.app button { font: inherit }` overrides the low-specificity 10px `.tab` declaration. Native uses these effective values. Display text follows the native large-title scaling curve; prose retains body scaling; this needs AX visual proof.
6. **System chrome:** actual status/safe-area geometry and system-owned sheet/keyboard affordances vary by OS/device. Record measured offsets; they do not excuse controllable content spacing, font, color, hierarchy or missing actions.
7. **Scoped status wording:** Plan ends with “That’s the latest saved on this device.” This provides the approved continuity ending without claiming all accounts are caught up; live freshness remains unverified. CloudKit publication is described as shared, never delivered or read. These are exact wording scopes, not blanket native adaptations.
8. **Global creation:** Plan retains + because original Create/iPad annotations require a global ambiguous-intent entry, although the open-Plan illustration omits it. Table/Recipes use direct contextual creation. The photo overlay says Change photo rather than the mock’s Edit photo; it replaces the attachment and sits beside Remove. The annotation requires on-photo edit/remove placement, not a crop editor.
