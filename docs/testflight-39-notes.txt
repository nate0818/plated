Household joining and profile recovery

- Recent household joins now appear in the bell even after an iCloud recovery refresh. A new invitation after removal has its own join notification.
- Recover household profile photos even when the phone already has the current record version.
- Refresh from iCloud no longer incorrectly tells you to invite an existing member again.

Please update both phones. If you are already in the same household, stay joined. On each phone, open Table and pull down to refresh. Check each other's profile photo and a newly shared dish. Personal dinner history from before joining remains private.
