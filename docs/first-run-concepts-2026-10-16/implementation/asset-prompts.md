# Production asset generation

Tool: built-in image_gen. Source reference: ../icon-study.png, option B.
Export: icon PNGs resized to 1024 × 1024, no alpha; welcome exported as JPEG.
Default and Dark intentionally share the same cream-on-green icon. Tinted has
a separate monochrome image. These are flat asset-catalog icons, not an Icon
Composer layered document.

## Default / Dark icon

Refine ONLY option B, the right-hand custom Plated P, into ONE final square production iOS app icon image. Output 1024 by 1024 square artwork. Remove the entire board, captions, other icons and white surround. Background must fill the WHOLE square edge-to-edge with perfectly flat solid #191C17 deep green: no pre-rounded corners, no transparency, no gradient, no texture, no shadow. One crisp flat solid cream #EDF0E6 custom P centered optically, occupies about 62 percent image height with generous padding. Preserve B's distinctive sculptural serif shape, broad vertical stem, gently flared foot, rounded bowl, open curling lower bowl junction. Refine curves smooth and balanced, thicken delicate joins for home-screen legibility, no extra flourish. Precisely two colors apart from antialiasing. This is final standalone icon artwork, NOT a mockup or presentation. No text except the P itself.

## Tinted icon

Make an exact grayscale tinted-appearance variant of this final app icon. Preserve the identical P silhouette, position, proportions and all negative space. Change only colors: pure black edge-to-edge square background, pure white flat P, smooth antialiased edge pixels. No rounding of image corners, no borders, no shadow, no gradient, no texture. Square 1024x1024 PNG artwork.

At upper right a cropped adult hand serves pasta from a black pan. No faces, no text, no logos. Warm natural window light, believable imperfect domestic scene, restrained muted colors, food appetizing and in sharp focus, candid food magazine quality. Keep both bowls and serving hand in the central two thirds so a modest wide crop retains the story. No fancy flowers, decorative props or extravagant styling.
